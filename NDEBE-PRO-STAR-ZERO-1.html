HEDGE ANALYZER — UNDER 4 + OVER 5
Ranks every market for a simultaneous Under 4 + Over 5 pair. The pair only loses when the next digit is 4 or 5 (probability D), so markets are ranked by how far D sits below the live breakeven. Payouts are read live from Deriv — nothing is hard-coded.
Maths: pair profit per $1 per leg = (p − 1) − D × (p + 1). Breakeven D* = (p − 1) / (p + 1). A random market has D = 20%. Checks adjust the score and the tag; they never block a result, so a best market is always shown. Historical research only — not a guarantee of the next tick.

HIGHER / LOWER ANALYZER — BEST MARKET + DIRECTION
Reads each market's last 1000 prices with five indicators — displacement, trend efficiency, EMA cross, tick agreement and window agreement — then back-tests the trend rule on the same history. Ranks markets with sufficient price history and picks HIGHER or LOWER. Contract offsets are discovered dynamically from each market's observed tick movement and accepted only after Deriv returns live proposals for both signed sides. No assumed offsets are used.
Maths: with tick size σ (std of price changes over the last 500 ticks), the move over n ticks has zn = (Pnow − Pnow−n) / (σ√n), computed for n = 50, 100, 250, 500. A random walk gives z near 0, so a large |z| is a real drift. Efficiency ER = |net move| / total path length (1 = straight line, 0 = pure chop). EMA cross = (EMA20 − EMA100) / σ. Tick agreement = share of up ticks in the last 100, tested against 50%. Window agreement = how many of the four windows point the same way. Back-test: every time |z100| ≥ 1, follow it and check the price 5 ticks later; the hit rate is shown against 50%. Checks adjust the score and tag; they never block a result. Historical research only.

RISE / FALL ANALYZER — BEST MARKET + DIRECTION
Studies each market's own last 1000 prices. For RISE and for FALL it learns, per market state (5-tick momentum and recent volatility), how often a 5-tick contract lost, counting a tie as a loss. It ranks every market by how much safer the current state is than that market's normal, and picks RISE or FALL. No barrier and no payouts are requested.
Reading it: "State loss" is the share of past 5-tick contracts that lost from the same state; "normal" is that market's average loss for the same side. Advantage = normal − state loss. Stable means the advantage held in both halves of the history. Typical Rise/Fall payouts are under 100%, so a side must win clearly more than about half the time to profit — check the live payout in DTrader. Historical research only; synthetic indices are random, so no past pattern is guaranteed to repeat.
